About Exo-Sieve

AI-Driven Exoplanet Discovery

Exo-Sieve is a two-stage artificial intelligence pipeline designed for exoplanet discovery. It combines unsupervised anomaly detection with intelligent classification, filtering through raw light curve data to identify promising planetary candidates.

Traditional detection methods rely on manual analysis and large-scale filtering, but Exo-Sieve automates the process, reducing noise and increasing efficiency. By leveraging deep learning and anomaly detection, the system can highlight rare events that may indicate the presence of exoplanets.

The project is inspired by NASA's missions, including Kepler, K2, and TESS, providing scientists and enthusiasts with an accessible platform to explore cosmic data.

Stage 1

Anomaly detection filters unusual signals from noisy data.

Stage 2

AI classifier evaluates filtered signals for exoplanet signatures.

Impact

Speeds up discovery and reduces false positives in astronomy research.
